Summary

Computational biologist with six years of research experience building, validating and benchmarking integrative multi-omics and network-biology pipelines for understanding disease pathophysiology, biomarker identification, drug-target prediction and drug repurposing. First-authored a variant-to-gene mapping and target prioritization method that outperformed established tools, including Open Targets, PoPS and FUMA, and uncovered population-specific disease manifestations in PCOS. Strong foundation in Python and R, with skills in data integration, standardization, statistical analysis and machine learning. Currently exploring how AI-agent automation can accelerate biomarker identification and target identification for clinical-trial development, aiming toward precision medicine for complex diseases.

Research Experience

PhD Researcher, Computational Biology, NIT Durgapur, India2020-Present
Thesis: Elucidating the Pathophysiology of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome via an Integrative Multi-omics Approach. Supervisor: Dr. Debojyoti De. Thesis submitted Sept 2026 (under revision).
  • Applied integrated multi-omics evidence and a network-biology framework on PCOS-associated variants, scored genes by variant influence, prioritized targets across ancestries in 7 disease-relevant tissues, and uncovered population-specific differences in PCOS disease manifestation. Benchmarked against L2G, FUMA, MAGMA, PoPS and Open Targets. East Asian PCOS is more metabolic, whereas European PCOS is more dominated by immune functions. Validated output on independent patient transcriptomes with GSEA.
  • Nominated genetically supported drug-repurposing candidates, with one candidate independently corroborated by a PCOS clinical trial of anakinra.
  • Explored the genetic correlation between PCOS and immune/metabolic diseases, observing the same ancestry-specific pattern in East Asian and European cohorts; extending this through exposure-outcome Mendelian randomization (ongoing).
  • Working on a neurodevelopmental disorders project to understand the causal variants and effects underlying autism spectrum disorder (ASD).
  • Studying host-microbiome interactions, linking the microbiome with human genetics in disease context.
  • Mentored 5 MTech and 1 MSc student in omics analysis and scientific writing (1 MTech student ongoing); communicated findings through peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations.
